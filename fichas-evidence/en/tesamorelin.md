# GHRH ANALOG (GROWTH HORMONE RELEASER)
# Tesamorelin

The exception on the GH axis: the only growth hormone releaser with regulatory approval, published phase 3 trials and a real label. Everything CJC-1295 and sermorelin promise, tesamorelin had to demonstrate.

**LEVEL A**
Phase 3 randomized trials, published, replicated and FDA-approved — for one specific indication.

## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Reduces visceral abdominal fat (in HIV lipodystrophy) — LEVEL A**
Phase 3: ~15–18% reduction in visceral fat at 26–52 weeks versus placebo. It is the approved indication — and the effect reverses on stopping.

**Reduces liver fat (in people with HIV) — LEVEL A · 1 trial**
Published randomized trial with reduced liver fat. No approved indication for this.

**Improves cognition — MIXED RESULTS**
Small studies with signals in some subgroups; nothing conclusive or approved.

**Works for abdominal aesthetics in the general population — NO DATA**
All the evidence is in HIV lipodystrophy. Extrapolation to "burning belly fat" in healthy people has never been tested.

---

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
Phase 3 trials in people with HIV and visceral fat accumulation from antiretroviral treatment. The group treated with 2 mg daily lost a significant portion of deep visceral fat versus placebo, measured by imaging. When the drug was stopped, the fat came back — it is a maintenance treatment, not a cure.

**LIMITATIONS**
A specific population (HIV with lipodystrophy): results do not automatically transfer to the general population. Manufacturer-funded.

**ORIGINAL CITATION**
Phase 3 trials of tesamorelin in HIV-associated lipodystrophy
New England Journal of Medicine, 2007 (pivotal program)
Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled · ~400 participants

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND INTERACTIONS
There is an approved label here — and it applies to anyone using unapproved GHRH analogs too:

**Active cancer — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**Disruption of the hypothalamic-pituitary axis (surgery, radiation, pituitary tumor) — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**Pregnancy — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**Diabetes or glucose intolerance — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Raises glucose; the label requires monitoring.

**Fluid retention, joint pain, carpal tunnel — CLASS SIGNAL**
Reported in the trials; generally reversible.

**Requires IGF-1 monitoring — LABEL**
The prescriber must watch that the growth factor does not spike.

## REGULATORY STATUS

**UNITED STATES · FDA** — Approved (Egrifta, 2010; new formulations since 2025) for reduction of visceral fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy. Any other use is off-label.

**COLOMBIA · INVIMA / MEXICO · COFEPRIS** — No known marketing authorization to date. In the region almost everything circulates via compounding or as "research material."

**EUROPE · EMA** — No current centralized authorization.

**SPORT · WADA** — Prohibited (growth hormone releaser), even though it is an approved drug.
