## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
A randomized placebo-controlled trial in healthy adults showed that one or two injections raised GH and IGF-1 in a sustained way for days or weeks. The study measured hormones and short-term safety. It did not measure muscle, fat, performance or any outcome that matters to the person buying it.

**LIMITATIONS**
Dozens of participants, weeks of follow-up, purely hormonal endpoints, and a program the manufacturer itself later abandoned.

**ORIGINAL CITATION**
Prolonged stimulation of growth hormone and IGF-I secretion by CJC-1295
Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism, 2006
Randomized, placebo-controlled trial in healthy adults

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND SIGNALS
No label exists. GH-axis signals (shared with approved releasers):

**Active cancer or recent history — STRONG CAUTION**
Elevated IGF-1 is a cell growth factor. Approved GH products contraindicate it in active malignancy.

**Diabetes or prediabetes — CAUTION**
The GH axis raises glucose. A documented class effect in approved analogs.

**Fluid retention, joint pain, carpal tunnel — CLASS SIGNAL**

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding — NO DATA AT ALL**
