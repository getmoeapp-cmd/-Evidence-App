## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Speeds tendon and muscle healing — LEVEL C**
Consistent in animal models (rodents). Zero controlled human trials.

**Protects and repairs the gut — LEVEL C**
The compound's strongest preclinical base. A phase 2 in ulcerative colitis was started; its results never appeared in a peer-reviewed journal.

**Reduces inflammation and joint pain — NO CONTROLLED DATA**
A 12-patient report using bladder injections (2024) and uncontrolled case series. Spontaneous improvement, placebo and reporting bias could explain everything observed.

**Protects the brain / repairs nerves — LEVEL C**
Animals only.

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
A 2025 systematic review analyzed 36 BPC-157 studies published between 1993 and 2024. It found 35 preclinical and only 1 clinical. The only published intravenous safety pilot in humans had 2 participants. That is the entire human map.

**LIMITATIONS**
Most of the animal literature comes from a single research group (Croatia), with little independent replication — a methodological red flag in any field.

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND SIGNALS
No label and no human safety base exist. The following are theoretical mechanism signals, not clinical data.

**Active cancer or recent history — THEORETICAL CAUTION**
BPC-157 stimulates angiogenesis (VEGF) in animal models — the same mechanism tumors use to grow. Never studied in humans with cancer.

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding — NO DATA AT ALL**

**Drug interactions — NO DATA AT ALL**

## REGULATORY STATUS

**UNITED STATES · FDA** — Not approved. Restricted from compounding in 2023 (Category 2, safety concerns); in 2026 the FDA began reclassification and committee review. Coming off a restricted list is not approval: there is still no indication, no validated dose and no human efficacy data.

**COLOMBIA · INVIMA / MEXICO · COFEPRIS** — No marketing authorization. Circulates only as a "research reagent."

**SPORT · WADA** — Prohibited. It will show up on a doping test.
