## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Reduces anxiety — LEVEL B**
Russian trials in generalized anxiety disorder, including a comparison with a benzodiazepine. It is the registered indication and the best-supported claim.

**Reduces fatigue / neurasthenia — LEVEL B**
A registered indication in Russia; the neurasthenia diagnosis does not exist in current US diagnostic criteria.

**Improves memory and focus in healthy people — NO DATA**
No trial of cognitive enhancement in a healthy population. It is the main use in the West and the least supported.

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
The most cited study followed 62 patients with anxiety and reported a reduction comparable to a benzodiazepine, without the characteristic sedation. It is an interesting result — with a small sample, in a single country, and nobody outside it has reproduced it.

**LIMITATIONS**
Placebo response rates in anxiety trials commonly run 30–40%, which makes small samples without rigorous blinding especially fragile.

**ANCHOR CITATION**
Russian trial of Selank in generalized anxiety disorder (~62 patients), compared with medazepam
Pharmaceutical registration in the Russian Federation (2009)

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND SIGNALS

**Use alongside psychiatric medication — NO DATA**
Interaction with SSRIs, benzodiazepines or other anxiolytics has not been studied.

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding — NO DATA**

**Long-term use — NO DATA**
The trials lasted weeks, not years.

**Nasal irritation** — the most reported adverse effect of the intranasal route.
