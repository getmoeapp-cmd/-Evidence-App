# Epitalon

## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Extends life / reduces mortality — LEVEL C**
Reported in Russian cohorts of older adults followed for years. No modern randomization, no verifiable blinding, no independent replication. It is the biggest claim with the weakest support.

**Lengthens telomeres — LEVEL C**
Observed in cell culture. Never demonstrated as a clinical outcome in humans.

**Regulates melatonin and sleep — LEVEL C**
Pineal mechanistic rationale; scarce human data.

**General anti-aging — NO CONTROLLED DATA**

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
The core evidence is follow-up studies in older Russian adults reporting lower mortality in the treated groups. They were published mostly in Russian journals, by the same group that developed the compound, and nobody has independently reproduced them in more than thirty years.

**LIMITATIONS**
When a single laboratory produces all the positive evidence for a compound, that is a methodological red flag in any field — not proof that it is false, but a reason not to treat it as established.

**ANCHOR CITATION**
Khavinson group cohort studies in older adults (St. Petersburg)
Publications mostly in Russian · no independent replication

## REGULATORY STATUS

**UNITED STATES · FDA** — Not approved. Restricted from compounding in 2023 (Category 2); included in the 2026 committee review. No approved indication.

**RUSSIA** — Related peptide products exist on the Russian market; that authorization is not equivalent to FDA, EMA, INVIMA or COFEPRIS approval.

**COLOMBIA · INVIMA / MEXICO · COFEPRIS** — No marketing authorization. Circulates as a "research reagent."
