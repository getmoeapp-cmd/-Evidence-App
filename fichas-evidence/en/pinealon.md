## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
All the relevant literature comes from the same Russian institute that created the family of short "peptide bioregulators." The papers describe neuronal protection in cells and animals under stress. Nobody outside that group has reproduced it, and not one randomized trial in people exists.

**LIMITATIONS**
When all of a compound's positive evidence comes from those who developed and commercialize it, the evidence is not nullified — but it cannot be treated as established either.
