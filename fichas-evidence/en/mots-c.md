## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Improves metabolism and insulin sensitivity — LEVEL C**
Consistent in mice, including high-fat-diet models. Zero human trials.

**Mimics or enhances exercise — LEVEL C**
In humans, all that has been observed is that endogenous levels rise with training — correlation, not treatment.

**Helps with fat loss — NO HUMAN DATA**

**Longevity / mitochondrial function — LEVEL C**
A real and interesting laboratory mechanism. No evidence of a clinical outcome in people.

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
The furthest this compound got in humans was a phase 1 of a pharmaceutical analog for fatty liver and obesity. The company developing it wound down operations and never published efficacy data. Everything else is mouse, cell or correlation.

**LIMITATIONS**
A clinical program dying for financial reasons does not prove the compound fails — but it leaves the human map exactly where it was: empty.

**ANCHOR CITATION**
Phase 1 of the CB4211 analog (fatty liver/obesity) — program discontinued without published efficacy results

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND SIGNALS
No human safety base exists. Theoretical signals:

**Diabetes or glucose-lowering medication — THEORETICAL CAUTION**
If it does in people what it does in mice, it would move glucose and insulin. Nobody has measured it.

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding — NO DATA AT ALL**
