## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Improves skin appearance (wrinkles, elasticity) — LEVEL B · topical**
Small controlled cream studies showed improvements versus vehicle. Industry funding and small samples: real but weak evidence.

**Stimulates hair growth — LEVEL C**
Preclinical work and commercial formulations; no published independent randomized trials.

**Wound healing — LEVEL C**
Broad animal base; human data limited to small series.

**"Resets" gene expression / systemic anti-aging — NO HUMAN DATA**
The claim comes from in vitro gene expression studies. Nobody has demonstrated a systemic effect from injecting GHK-Cu into people.

---

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
The best human data are 8–12 week facial cream studies: the treated side showed measurable improvements in elasticity and appearance versus the control side. None of that says what happens when the compound is injected.

**LIMITATIONS**
Small samples, partly subjective cosmetic endpoints, and funding from the industry that sells the creams.

**ANCHOR CITATION**
Controlled studies of topical GHK-Cu on facial skin (8–12 weeks)
Injectable route: no published human studies

---

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND SIGNALS

**Topical route** — Generally well tolerated as a cosmetic; local irritation possible.

**Injectable route — NO SAFETY DATA AT ALL**
Blood pressure, copper load, interactions: nobody has measured them in people.

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding — NO DATA** (any systemic route)

---

## REGULATORY STATUS

**TOPICAL COSMETIC** — Legal as a cosmetic ingredient. In Colombia, topical cosmetic peptides enter under Mandatory Health Notification (Andean Community Decision 516).

**INJECTABLE · UNITED STATES FDA** — Not approved. The injectable route was restricted from compounding in 2023 (Category 2) and included in the 2026 reclassification. No approved indication.

**COLOMBIA · INVIMA / MEXICO · COFEPRIS** — Not registered as a medicine. The injectable circulates only as a "research reagent."
