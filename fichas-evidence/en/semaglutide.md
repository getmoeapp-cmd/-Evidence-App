## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Reduces body weight in obesity — LEVEL A**
STEP 1: 14.9% loss at 68 weeks on 2.4 mg vs 2.4% on placebo. STEP UP: 20.7% at 72 weeks on the newer 7.2 mg dose.

**Improves glycemic control in type 2 diabetes — LEVEL A**
SUSTAIN (injectable) and PIONEER (oral) programs: HbA1c reductions superior to placebo and to active comparators.

**Reduces major cardiovascular events — LEVEL A · superiority demonstrated**
SELECT (17,604 patients): fewer cardiovascular deaths, heart attacks and strokes vs placebo in people with cardiovascular disease and overweight or obesity, without diabetes. FDA-approved indication (2024; oral route 2025). The only molecule in this category in Evidence with demonstrated cardiovascular superiority over placebo.

**Protects the kidney in chronic kidney disease with diabetes — LEVEL A**
FLOW: less kidney progression and death. Approved indication (2025).

**Treats fatty liver with inflammation (MASH) — LEVEL A**
ESSENCE: resolution of steatohepatitis vs placebo. Approved in 2025 for MASH with moderate-to-advanced fibrosis — the first liver indication in this category.

**Prevents or treats Alzheimer's — NOT SHOWN**
The phase 3 trials (EVOKE) missed their primary endpoint (2025). A promising mechanism is not the same as a positive result.

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
A 68-week trial in adults with overweight or obesity, without diabetes. The treated group lost substantially more body weight than placebo (14.9% vs 2.4%). The most frequent side effects were gastrointestinal — nausea, diarrhea, vomiting — mostly transient and concentrated during titration.

**LIMITATIONS**
All participants also received lifestyle counseling. The trial was funded by the manufacturer (Novo Nordisk).

**ORIGINAL CITATION**

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND INTERACTIONS

**Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**Multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN2) — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**Pregnancy — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**History of pancreatitis — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Exclusion criterion in the pivotal trials, so no data exists for this group.

**Gastroparesis or severe gastrointestinal disease — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Approved product label (delayed gastric emptying)

**Diabetic retinopathy (in people with T2D) — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Signal observed in SUSTAIN-6 from rapid glucose lowering. Approved product label

**Gallbladder disease — MONITORED SIGNAL**
Gallbladder events reported in trials; seek care for severe abdominal pain.

**Ischemic optic neuropathy (NAION) — MONITORED SIGNAL**
Very rare. Added to the European label in 2025 after reports of sudden vision loss. Estimated frequency is extremely low, but it is the most discussed new signal for this molecule.

**Insulin or sulfonylureas — INTERACTION**
Hypoglycemia risk; requires supervised adjustment. Approved product label

## REGULATORY STATUS

**UNITED STATES · FDA** — Approved. Ozempic: type 2 diabetes (2017) and chronic kidney disease (2025). Wegovy: obesity (2021), cardiovascular risk (2024), MASH (2025), high dose 7.2 mg (2026). Oral Wegovy 25 mg (late 2025). Oral tablets for T2D with a cardiovascular indication (2025).

**COLOMBIA · INVIMA** — Ozempic registered for type 2 diabetes since 2019. Wegovy registered for obesity since 2024.

**MEXICO · COFEPRIS** — Approved (Ozempic and Wegovy).

**EUROPE · EMA** — Approved (Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus).

This is the most counterfeited molecule in the category: INVIMA has issued alerts over compounded and social-media "semaglutide" with no marketing authorization. Registration does not mean the thing sold to you on Instagram is the registered product.
