## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
KPV is the terminal fragment of the hormone α-MSH. In cells and in mice it blocks key inflammatory pathways without causing skin darkening, the effect that limits the full molecule. Nobody has tested that in a person in a controlled study.

**LIMITATIONS**
A three-amino-acid peptide with good theoretical bioavailability is still, in humans, an untested hypothesis.

**ANCHOR CITATION**
Preclinical literature on KPV in intestinal and cutaneous inflammation models
No published human clinical trials
