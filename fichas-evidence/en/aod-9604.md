## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Burns fat / causes weight loss — NOT SHOWN**
Phase 2a (12 weeks): 2.6 kg versus 0.8 kg on placebo — a real signal. Phase 2b (24 weeks, ~536 people): no statistically significant difference versus placebo. The program was terminated because of it.

**Does not raise IGF-1 or affect glucose — LEVEL B**
Confirmed in humans. This is its real mechanistic advantage: the one claim the trials do support.

**Repairs cartilage / knee osteoarthritis — LEVEL C**
A small 2014 study and animal models. No phase 3. The current benchmark is semaglutide, which did reduce knee pain in a large trial.

**"Spot fat burning" (belly, chin) — NO DATA**
No study has tested a local effect from a subcutaneous injection.

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
The decisive study was a 24-week phase 2b with more than 500 adults with obesity, designed specifically to answer whether AOD-9604 caused weight loss. The answer was no: no significant difference versus placebo. The company closed the program in 2007.

**LIMITATIONS**
Safety was well documented (one of the best safety datasets in the peptide space). Efficacy was not.

**ANCHOR CITATION**
Metabolic Pharmaceuticals clinical program (phase 2a 2001 · phase 2b 2007)
~536 participants in the pivotal trial · primary endpoint not met

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND SIGNALS

**Safety profile — CLEAN in trials**
Well tolerated across more than 900 participants, with no IGF-1 elevation, no insulin resistance, no fluid retention.

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding — NO DATA**

**A note on honesty:** being safe does not make something effective. This compound is the perfect demonstration of that difference.
