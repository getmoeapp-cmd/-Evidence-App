## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Reduces body weight in obesity — LEVEL A**
Published phase 2 (up to 24.2% at 48 weeks). Phase 3 topline: up to 28.3% at 80 weeks and 30.3% at 104 weeks — bariatric surgery range.

**Improves glycemic control in type 2 diabetes — LEVEL A**
Published phase 2 (dose-dependent HbA1c reductions). TRIUMPH-2 topline: up to 20.8% weight loss in people with T2D.

**Reduces liver fat (fatty liver / MASLD) — LEVEL B**
Phase 2 substudy with reductions in liver fat in most participants. No formal phase 3 indication yet.

**Reduces knee osteoarthritis pain — LEVEL B**
TRIUMPH-4 topline (2026): up to 28.7% weight loss with pain reduction. Not peer-reviewed.

**Reduces major cardiovascular events — NOT SHOWN**
The cardiovascular event analysis in TRIUMPH-3 was inconclusive. It improved risk markers (non-HDL cholesterol, blood pressure), which is not the same as reducing events.

**Effects on cancer — NO HUMAN DATA**
Preclinical research only. No human trials with this endpoint.

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
A 48-week trial in adults with obesity or overweight, without diabetes. The highest-dose group lost an average of 24.2% of body weight — the largest reduction reported in an obesity trial at the time. The most frequent adverse effects were gastrointestinal and increased with dose. A transient rise in heart rate was also observed.

**LIMITATIONS**
338 participants — small next to a phase 3 trial. Funded by the manufacturer (Eli Lilly). The 2026 phase 3 data comes from company press releases and has not been peer-reviewed.

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND INTERACTIONS
No approved label exists. Everything below comes from trial exclusion criteria and GLP-1 class effects.

**Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma — DO NOT USE**
GLP-1 class warning · trial exclusion criterion

**Multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN2) — DO NOT USE**
GLP-1 class warning · trial exclusion criterion

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding — DO NOT USE**
Exclusion criterion in all trials

**History of pancreatitis — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Exclusion criterion in the pivotal trials: no data exists for this group.

**Gastroparesis or severe gastrointestinal disease — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Class effect (delayed gastric emptying)

**Diabetic retinopathy (in people with T2D) — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Class effect from rapid glucose lowering

**Insulin or sulfonylureas — INTERACTION**
Hypoglycemia risk; requires supervised adjustment.

**MONITORED SIGNALS (phase 3, 2026)**
Dysesthesia (altered skin sensation) and urinary tract infections reported in TRIUMPH-1 — mostly mild to moderate, most resolving during treatment. Vomiting in up to 25.3% at the highest dose. Discontinuation due to adverse events: 4.1%–11.3%, rising with dose.
