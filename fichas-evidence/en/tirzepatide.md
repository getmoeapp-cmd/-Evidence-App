## WHAT'S CLAIMED · WHAT'S BEEN MEASURED

**Reduces body weight in obesity — LEVEL A**
SURMOUNT-1: up to 20.9% loss at 72 weeks vs 3.1% on placebo. In SURMOUNT-5, head to head, it outperformed semaglutide — the only approved drug that has.

**Improves glycemic control in type 2 diabetes — LEVEL A**
The full SURPASS program: HbA1c reductions superior to placebo and to active comparators.

**Treats obstructive sleep apnea — LEVEL A**
SURMOUNT-OSA: substantial reduction in apnea events. First drug therapy approved by the FDA for this indication (2024).

**Reduces major cardiovascular events — LEVEL A, with a caveat**
SURPASS-CVOT (NEJM 2025, 13,299 patients, 4 years): noninferior to dulaglutide — a drug that already reduces events on its own. Superiority on the composite of CV death, heart attack and stroke was not reached (p=0.09). All-cause mortality was 16% lower. Translation: protects the heart at least as much as a proven GLP-1; "better than" is not yet demonstrated.

**Reduces liver fat (MASH / fatty liver) — LEVEL B**
Phase 2 trial with resolution of steatohepatitis in most treated patients. No approved indication.

**Improves heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) — LEVEL A · 1 trial**
SUMMIT (NEJM 2024): fewer heart failure events in people with obesity. Indication not yet approved.

## THE SOURCE

**IN PLAIN LANGUAGE**
A 72-week trial in adults with obesity or overweight, without diabetes. The maximum-dose group lost an average of 20.9% of body weight versus 3.1% on placebo. The most frequent side effects were gastrointestinal — nausea, diarrhea, constipation — mostly mild to moderate and concentrated during dose escalation.

**LIMITATIONS**
All participants also received lifestyle counseling. The trial was funded by the manufacturer (Eli Lilly).

## CONTRAINDICATIONS AND INTERACTIONS

**Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**Multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN2) — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**Pregnancy — DO NOT USE**
Approved product label

**History of pancreatitis — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Not studied in this group: excluded from the pivotal trials.

**Gastroparesis or severe gastrointestinal disease — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Approved product label (delayed gastric emptying)

**Diabetic retinopathy (in people with T2D) — NEEDS SUPERVISION**
Approved product label

**Gallbladder disease — MONITORED SIGNAL**
Gallbladder events reported in trials; seek care for severe abdominal pain.

**Insulin or sulfonylureas — INTERACTION**
Hypoglycemia risk; requires supervised adjustment. Approved product label

**Oral contraceptives — INTERACTION**
Delayed gastric emptying may reduce their effectiveness. The label recommends a backup method for 4 weeks after starting and after each dose increase. Specific to tirzepatide.
